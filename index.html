01 / Research

Algebraic models
for higher structures.

From classical constructions to the world of ∞-categories.

How can explicit algebraic constructions retain the higher information that matters in homotopy theory?

My research is in algebraic topology and homotopy theory, with an emphasis on categorical and higher-categorical methods. I am interested in the relationship between higher structures and concrete homological models.

In joint work with Dr. Ettore Aldrovandi, I study an extension of Mac Lane’s cubical Q-construction to exact ∞-categories, building on McCarthy’s construction for exact categories. We use cubical skeletal filtrations to construct coherent chain complexes of cubical objects valued in a stable ∞-category.

Current directions

Normalization and explicit differentials; comparisons with classical chain models; and connections to algebraic K-theory and topological Hochschild homology.

A more technical overview

Mac Lane’s cubical Q-complex computes the stable homology of abelian groups. My work studies the passage from exact ∞-categorical diagrams to coherent chain complexes, using the cubical skeletal filtration. After passing to the homotopy category, alternating sums of the recovered face maps give the associated ordinary Q′-complex. Recovering the desired classical chain models directly from the coherent construction is part of the ongoing investigation.

I am also interested in filtered objects and possible connections with persistence theory. These are developing directions rather than completed comparisons with the classical invariants.
